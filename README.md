# Bryce Kristensen

**Media & marketing executive · Founder, [Powell Street Media](https://powellstreetmedia.com)** · San Francisco Bay Area

I build content operations that didn't exist yet, then make them run at scale. At NBCUniversal I built and ran the NBCU Digital Lab, the company's first original digital content studio: a 75-person team with an $11M P&L producing 500 episodes a year, and the group that led NBCU's programming for its $500M Snapchat partnership. Today I lead content, digital and paid media for Habitat for Humanity Greater San Francisco.

I'm constantly pushing the boundaries of how the connections only people can create and the most thoughtful uses of AI can work together.

### How I work with AI

- **I design the tools myself.** I build bespoke agents for brand voice, paid media, content and analytics, and own them end to end, from model choice and prompt design to evaluation and cost.
- **Automation with guardrails.** For example, a Google Ads system that monitors campaigns, shifts budget within set limits and flags recommendations for a person to act on.
- **People keep the judgment.** AI speeds up testing, optimization and reporting. The storytelling and the final calls stay human.

Most of that work is client-side and private. Public experiments will show up here over time.
